mailxray was published under the MIT license for its first two commits
(`acc3696`, and `docs: full English README …`). From commit
`license: PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0` onward it is licensed under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

What this means in practice:

- **Anything obtained under MIT stays MIT.** A license already granted cannot be
  revoked: if you cloned or forked the repository while it was MIT, that copy
  remains under MIT for you.
- **Anything obtained from now on** is PolyForm Noncommercial: free for personal
  and other noncommercial use, commercial use requires a
  [commercial license](COMMERCIAL.md).
- No future release will be dual-licensed back to MIT; the project moves forward
  under PolyForm Noncommercial plus optional commercial terms.

The change was made by the sole copyright holder, so no third-party consent was
required. Contributions from here on are accepted under
